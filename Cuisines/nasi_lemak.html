Nasi Lemak

Malaysia's National Dish

Back to Home

History

Nasi Lemak dates back to the 19th century, when Malay farmers needed a hearty breakfast. The dish was simple and practical — rice steamed in coconut milk, wrapped in banana leaves for field workers.

Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups of Jasmine rice
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 2 pandan leaves
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Dried anchovies (ikan bilis)
  • Roasted peanuts
  • Hard boiled eggs
  • Sambal

Steps

  1. Wash rice until water runs clear. Cook with coconut milk, pandan leaves and salt.
  2. Fry dried anchovies until golden and crispy.
  3. Prepare sambal by frying blended chili paste until thick.
  4. Serve rice on a banana leaf with all toppings.

Fun Facts

  • Nasi Lemak is served on Malaysia Airlines flights as a symbol of Malaysian identity.
  • The word "lemak" means "rich" or "fat" in Malay, referring to the coconut milk.
  • It is eaten for breakfast, lunch and dinner!
